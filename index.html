Axiom

Axiom for Android

The school app for teachers and parents. Sign in with the two codes your school gave you.

Download the app

Android 8 or newer

  1. Tap Download the app, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from this browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open Axiom, choose Teacher or Parent, and enter your school code and your own code.
  4. When a new version is ready, the app shows Update. Tap it and install over the old one; you stay signed in.
